# Phase 9 — Public Portfolio & Talent Showcase Engine Specification

## Overview
Generates public portfolio pages summarizing verified achievements, ELO ratings, radar charts, and tournament history.

---

## 1. Portfolio Components
- **Identity Header**: Initials, Level Badge, Rank Tier, Guild Tag.
- **Skill Radar Matrix**: Multi-axis SADL radar chart (UI, Architecture, Security, State, Performance).
- **Verified Certificates**: Blockchain-authenticated certificates with verification QR codes.
- **Experience Timeline**: Milestones achieved in Battle Grid & Academy.
