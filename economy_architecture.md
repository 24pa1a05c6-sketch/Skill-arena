# Phase 8 — Digital Economy & Economy Architecture Specification

## Economy System Overview
Skill Arena's Digital Economy is anchored around `$ARENA` Tokens, XP balances, Arena Coins, and digital assets available in The Vault Marketplace.

---

## 1. Currency Formulations
- **\$ARENA Tokens**: Primary platform currency earned via Battle Grid tournaments and top-ups.
- **XP**: Experience points earned via course completion, streaks, and tournament matches.
- **Arena Coins**: Secondary non-crypto reward currency for daily missions and referral bonuses.

---

## 2. Rarity Tiers & Asset Catalog
- **COMMON**: Standard assets available to all registered competitors.
- **RARE**: Unlocked via Battle Pass progressions (150 - 250 `$ARENA`).
- **EPIC**: Limited edition SADL glass skins & custom sound packs (250 - 500 `$ARENA`).
- **LEGENDARY**: Animated profile frames & Elite Season Passes (500+ `$ARENA`).
