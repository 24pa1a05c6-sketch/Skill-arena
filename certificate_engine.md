# Phase 9 — Verified Certificate Engine Specification

## Overview
Generates tamper-proof blockchain-ready certificates verifying skill mastery and tournament placements.

---

## 1. Certificate Cryptographic Metadata
- `verificationHash`: SHA-256 hash (`0x94F2...C7A0`).
- `issueDate`: Timestamp of issuance.
- `verifierName`: Neural Examiner or Enterprise Organizer.
- `publicVerificationUrl`: Web verification link for employers.
